Saved accounts

Fill in the e-mail/username and password for each panel. Passwords are encrypted on this PC and never leave it. "Log in all" does what you would: on a panel that is signed out it presses "Entrar" to open the login window, waits for the Cloudflare check to pass by itself (it never touches that box), types the e-mail and password and presses "Entrar". A panel that is signed in is left alone. Nothing else in the game is touched.

Passwords cannot be saved on this computer (no encryption available). Names and e-mails can still be saved.

Diagnostics

The self-check looks at what the app can see by itself. The report is one block of text you can copy and send: it holds names of things, counts and true / false, never passwords, e-mails or numbers from the game.


      

        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      

    

  


  


  

    

      
Settings

      

      

        
Game panelsHow many accounts you play at the same time. The panels not in use are not loaded at all. Changing it reloads the window (you stay signed in).

        

      

      

        
AccountsThe saved logins of the game panels, used by Log in all (the passwords are encrypted).

        

      

      

        
Settings backupSave your settings to a file, or load them from one (on a new PC, after a fresh install). Saved logins and passwords are never in it, nor your history.

        
 

      

      

        
LanguageThe language of the whole app: the screens, the notifications and the tray menu.

        

      

      

        
Start with the PCOpens 4horizon by itself when you sign in to Windows, so that the alerts and the history keep going after a restart.

        

      

      

        
Minimize to trayMinimizing the window hides 4horizon in the tray, as closing it does. Click the tray icon to bring it back.

        

      

      

        
Disable GPU accelerationTurn this on if you see visual glitches when hovering or taking screenshots. Requires restarting 4horizon.

        

      

      

        
Light modeFor slower PCs. The game screens stop their decorative animations (the game's own "reduce motion": shine, glows, pulsing buttons), the Summary is redrawn every 5 seconds instead of every second, and 4horizon's own animations stop. The hunts, the numbers and the captures do not change. No restart needed.

        

      

      

        
Streamer modeFor streaming 4horizon (OBS: Window Capture) while you use other apps. The game screens keep moving when other windows cover 4horizon (not when it is minimized: Windows cannot capture a minimized window), and the e-mails of the logins are hidden on screen. It uses about as much CPU as a visible window, and economy mode is not available while it is on. Restart 4horizon after changing it.

        

      

      
NotificationsSaved

      

        
Windows notificationsAlerts that pop up on your screen, even when the window is hidden in the tray. The tray menu has the same switch.

        

      

      

        

          
Tell me whenUntick what you do not want to hear about.

          

            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
          

        

        

          
Great catchA catch counts as great by its IV and its quality. OR: it notifies when a catch reaches at least one of the two numbers. AND: it notifies only when a catch reaches both. The IV goes up to 192. The quality is the number after the × of a catch (Rara ×1.37, Lendária ×1.78). To ignore one of the two: with OR put a number above the highest possible, with AND put 0.

          

        

        

          
Great catch, 2nd ruleTick Active to have a second combination. A catch is great when it meets the rule above OR this one. Example: above IV 130 AND quality 1.75, here IV 170 OR quality 5 (no catch reaches 5, so only the IV counts).

          

        

        

          
Valuable dropThe price of ONE item at the NPC shop. An item worth less than this does not notify.

          

        

        

          
Supplies running lowWarns when the balls or the potions of an account will run out within this time at the pace of the current hunt. It needs some minutes of hunting to know the pace.

          

        

        

          
StoppedWarns when an account that is hunting gets no kills for this long, or leaves the hunt (the city, for example) and is not back within that time; and when nothing arrives from its game for the second time.

          

        

        

          
Quiet hoursNo alerts between these two times (the time of this computer). An alert that happens then is not shown later. A window like 23:00 to 07:00 crosses midnight. The test button still works.

          

            
            
            
          

        

        

          
Sound and startThe Windows notification sound, and the notice that tells you the alerts are on each time the app starts.

          

        

        

          
Try itShows a notification now, with the sound setting above.
